# Idea and positioning

## Product thesis

Vendora helps merchants turn conversations into correctly fulfilled orders and run the associated customer, stock and payment workflow from one dashboard. Customers browse and ask questions in WhatsApp. Sellers manage customer records, inventory, payments, dispatch and follow-ups, assisted by text and voice AI.

Suggested positioning: **Your AI workspace for conversations, customers and commerce.** This is proposed copy, not a cleared trademark or approved public promise.

## Users and problems

- Owner: misses chats while handling stock, payment screenshots and dispatch.
- Sales/support staff: need shared customer context and an assigned follow-up queue.
- Fulfilment staff: need correct variants, confirmed payment/COD state and packing details.
- Customer: wants an accurate answer, available product, trustworthy checkout and order updates.

Initial segment hypothesis: small apparel/accessories sellers with 100–1,000 monthly orders and 1–5 staff. India can be the pilot while brand, architecture and product vocabulary remain international. Initial customer segment is not yet accepted.

## Differentiation to prove

CRM and order management must be first-class workflows, not just chatbot logs. Voice administration must save time after corrections. Live variant/stock lookup, verified payments, a dispatch queue and readable customer timelines should form one coherent workflow.

Native Meta AI and existing platforms already offer overlapping features. Do not claim that AI replies or a CRM alone are unique. Measure operational outcomes: admin time, order mistakes, missed follow-ups and retained merchant usage.

## International readiness

Store country, locale, timezone and currency per merchant. Normalize phone identifiers; display local formatting. Store money in integer minor units with currency metadata and currency-aware rounding. Avoid assuming every currency has two decimal places. Use UTC timestamps and merchant-local displays. Localize messages independently of data schema. Quote provider rates by destination/category/currency/effective date. International payments, tax and language support are phased integrations, not launch promises.

## Scope boundaries

MVP is a merchant operating tool with a WhatsApp entry point. A public multi-seller marketplace, lending, logistics fleet, full accounting suite and unrestricted general-purpose assistant are outside the proposed MVP. Product photography and advanced analytics are later capabilities unless Ankush prioritizes them explicitly.
